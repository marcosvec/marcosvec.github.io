RPI Dorms Space — CAD Lead

View 3D Demo

What it is

A collaborative project to model RPI dorm rooms in accurate 3D — furniture, layout, dimensions, the works. I lead the CAD side, which means I'm responsible for building and maintaining the models, establishing modeling standards for the team, and making sure everything is dimensionally accurate enough to be actually useful.

My role

As CAD lead, I own the Siemens NX workflow — from initial geometry and parametric constraints to export pipelines that feed into the web viewer. That means keeping parts clean and organized so others on the team can pick up where I leave off without having to untangle a mess of suppressed features.

I also built the interactive 3D web viewer that lets you walk through the room, click on furniture, measure distances, and toggle between an assembled view and an exploded one. Getting CAD geometry into a browser in a usable state required a fair amount of mesh optimization and WebGL work.

What I learned

Managing a CAD project with multiple contributors is different from just modeling on your own. File structure, naming conventions, and parametric intent matter a lot more when someone else needs to modify your parts six weeks later. I got a lot better at thinking ahead on that front.

BikeSuite — Bike Shop POS

Visit bikesuite.net

What it is

BikeSuite is a point-of-sale and shop management system I've been building for bike shops, designed to compete with Ascend RMS — the dominant (and expensive) software most shops are stuck with. The idea came from working at Merrick Bicycles, where I do assembly, tune-ups, and repairs, and watching how clunky the existing software workflow is day-to-day.

Why I built it

Ascend RMS works, but it's slow, overpriced for small shops, and not designed by anyone who's actually worked a service counter. I wanted to build something that matches how a real shop actually operates — fast ticket creation, sensible inventory lookup, and repair tracking that doesn't require three clicks to find a work order.

Where it's at

The core POS and service ticket flow is functional. I'm currently working on integrating AI features — things like predictive maintenance alerts based on service history, dynamic pricing insights from parts costs, and customer lifetime value analysis. The goal is useful business intelligence, not conversational AI for the sake of it.

I've also been exploring ways to pull UI components from Uiverse.io into the interface and connect the whole thing to Claude via MCP for some of the backend intelligence features.

CAD Modeling Work

Overview

A collection of mechanical and aerospace parts and assemblies built over the course of my time at RPI. Some are coursework, some are personal, and some came out of the Dorms Space project. The common thread is trying to model things with real design intent — not just geometry that looks right, but geometry that would actually be manufacturable and modifiable.

What I've worked on

Multi-part mechanical assemblies with constrained degrees of freedom, aerospace components requiring tight tolerances, furniture models accurate enough to pull meaningful dimensions from, and technical drawings with proper GD&T annotations. SolidWorks for a lot of coursework, Siemens NX for the Dorms Space project and anything that needs serious parametric control.

Interactive 3D Dorm Room

Open

What it is

A browser-based 3D viewer built with Three.js for the Dorms Space project. You can orbit the room, click on individual pieces of furniture to get info, measure distances between points, and switch between a normal assembled view and an exploded view. It runs at a stable 60 FPS on most hardware, with adaptive quality that scales down for weaker devices.

The interesting parts

Getting the geometry pipeline right took most of the work — exporting from NX, cleaning meshes in Blender, and loading them efficiently in the browser without tanking performance. I built a priority-based loading system so the room feels responsive even before all the models finish downloading, and a memory manager that cleans up geometry and textures as the session goes on.
